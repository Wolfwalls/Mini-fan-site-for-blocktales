Pre-Prologue

The game opens on you (a robloxian) heading to an event where builderman/David Bazucki is going to talk about what will be changing in Robloxia, as you head through town you see many interesting faces, most importantly including an old penguin,whose name is Tutuorial Terry. You arrive to the event and are invited to sit next to the penguin. After builderman has gone through a little bit of his speech a Mysterious Figure jumps on to stage to take over the event. Tutorial Terry gets up to fight them and asks for his "old pal"(You) to help him fight and gives you a ball. You fight off the Mysterious Figure's minion and then you attempot to thwart the Mysterious Figure's plot by hitting the device with your ball which causes you to get sucked into The DeviceTM, sending you back in time.
